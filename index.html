Who we are

Independent in outlook. Community-focused in practice.

Neath Port Talbot Communities First Independents is the regional party focused on representing local communities across Neath Port Talbot.

We are part of the registered Wales Communities First Independent Party and describe ourselves as centre-left. Our aim is to give local people a stronger voice and to focus on practical issues that matter in their communities.
